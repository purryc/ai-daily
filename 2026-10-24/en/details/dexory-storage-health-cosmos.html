What inventory records cannot show

An inventory system can record how many cartons occupy a location without knowing that a box is open, a pallet protrudes or wrapping hangs loose. Dexory uses high-resolution images from autonomous robots already travelling warehouse aisles to inspect multiple rack levels. Storage Health presents suspected issues with their location, time and image so a team can inspect the evidence remotely before deciding where to intervene. The useful output is an exception tied to a place, not an undifferentiated patrol video that someone must watch from beginning to end. Agreement between stock records and physical quantity does not answer whether the goods are stored in an acceptable condition.

A second analysis for uncertain images

The October 21 change adds Cosmos Reason 2-2B. Dexory says it fine-tuned the model on its own warehouse images, compared 2B and 8B variants and selected the smaller model for production on accuracy and deployment-efficiency grounds. Natural-language prompts describe inspection questions, while the added analysis is useful when an existing model returns low confidence. Examples include open boxes, goods pulled forward and misaligned pallets. This is an additional component in an inspection chain, not a claim that conversation replaces every vision model. Lighting, packaging and rack arrangements can vary by warehouse, so a deployment decision needs the distribution of errors in the actual operating environment.

A prompt does not move a pallet

Cosmos Reason 2 is a vision-language model that accepts text and visual input and generates answers or explanations. Access to the model is different from having a functioning warehouse inspection service. Dexory still supplies the capture process, location association and route from a detection to an operator’s queue. For an inventory or safety lead, being able to return from an alert to the original image supports an informed response. A flagged picture is not proof that a hazard has been removed, nor does this integration establish automatic forklift control. Verification, assignment, closure and confirmation on a later scan remain parts of the warehouse’s operational process.

Additional work on an existing patrol route

For an existing Dexory customer, extra analysis can use images from the same patrol rather than requiring a separate visual round for each kind of anomaly. A new site must still account for robots, routes, connectivity and integration with its warehouse systems. Public material does not provide a complete price, per-category false-positive and missed-detection rates, or response times across comparable sites. A smaller parameter count therefore cannot be translated directly into lower operating costs. Industry coverage dated October 23 repeats the earlier announcement; this record retains the October 21 event. The operational question is whether staff can locate the specific rack position needing attention with less searching, not whether a model name constitutes another safety certification.
