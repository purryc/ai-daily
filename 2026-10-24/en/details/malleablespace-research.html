Make the material addressable

A chat can mix a quotation, several alternatives and a decision that remains open. Returning days later often means explaining those relationships again. MalleableSpace makes the pieces separately addressable on a canvas. Its paper describes document and media nodes, connections and Frames that help supply context to an agent. This is useful for work whose next question depends on how earlier material was organized. A researcher can point to the unresolved branch instead of rebuilding a long prompt. The tradeoff is ordinary organizational work: someone still has to decide which material belongs together and which provisional conclusion deserves to remain visible. A crowded canvas is not automatically a clear argument.

Changes have a review surface

The current Huabu handbook separates discussion from modification. Chat inspects the Space without changing it; Agent can create, edit, move, group, connect and remove items. A review card collects changes by conversation, with a before-state preview and individual or grouped keep/revert controls. Revert is conditional: a later edit by a person or another agent can make the earlier operation unsafe, in which case Huabu disables it. That behavior matters when several strands of work share an object. The visible review surface helps a person inspect a reorganization without implying that every external effect can also be undone. Reverting a canvas change is not evidence of rollback in a connected repository or service.

A study of continuing use

The October 22 paper is the new event. Its formative deployment followed seven experienced agent users for three months while the system continued to evolve. Participants chose their own work, so the study illuminates practices rather than establishing a controlled speed advantage for a fixed product version. That distinction leaves a useful design question: can a person return to a project and recognize both the evidence already gathered and the question still awaiting a decision? Notes that preserve uncertainty can be as useful as polished outputs. For someone preparing an argument or a design review, the visible organization may make continuation easier; the study does not establish that every novice will find the extra surface worth maintaining.

Use existing agents and local project files

The linked implementation is Huabu, which already has public source and desktop packages; the paper is not its first software launch. Setup starts with a local Home folder, a supported ACP agent or adapter, and a Space. The repository distinguishes the MIT-licensed source from the terms accompanying packaged releases. It also makes clear that model service is not bundled: connected agents retain their accounts, tools and permissions, and provider charges may apply. This makes the workspace plausible for people who already have a preferred agent. Local project storage still does not mean local-only processing, because relevant content can be sent to the configured model or service. The practical adoption decision includes that existing agent relationship as well as the canvas.
