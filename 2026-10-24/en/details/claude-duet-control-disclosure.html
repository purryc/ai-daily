A brief before the day starts

A small-business owner may spend the morning checking unpaid invoices, confirming appointments and answering customers, then copying those commitments into another list. Duet reads connected applications and brings selected items into a morning brief. A person can also speak or type a list of work to delegate. Built around Opus 5.5, it uses a cloud computer that can continue after the mobile app closes. Inputs therefore include connected business context as well as the immediate request; outputs can be a document, spreadsheet, presentation or decision awaiting approval. The proposed saving is less searching across applications for the next action. Accounting judgments and commitments to customers still belong to the owner.

Approval is tied to an action

The October 23 disclosure adds operational detail to the earlier preview announcement. Important actions show the destination, content and recipients. A separate check assesses whether the action follows the person’s intent. Purchases use a one-time wallet card restricted to a merchant and amount, with approval inside Link. Securities trading and transfers between bank accounts remain prohibited rather than becoming available through repeated approval. This makes delegation refer to a particular action instead of one blanket permission. The design still has a usability tradeoff: frequent requests can train someone to approve without reading. Anthropic says it is adjusting that cadence; the published description does not establish that it consistently asks at the right moments.

Connections and memory are different controls

The task list gives each job its own page so the owner can see progress and change direction. Connected applications are shared with Claude: disabling an app in one surface disables it in the other. That stops future access but does not erase previously learned information; memory needs a separate reset. This distinction matters when a project ends or a relationship changes. Removing permission to read tomorrow is different from removing facts saved yesterday. Credentials follow a separate path, which the vendor says keeps passwords, tokens and browser cookies outside the model’s working environment. The detailed disclosure still describes 1Password as forthcoming, despite its presence among partner logos.

Background work shares the existing allowance

This remains the research preview placed on a waitlist on October 21, not a second product launch. Initial access is for US Pro and Max subscribers through iOS, Android and the web; Team and Enterprise are not included. Background checks consume the same allowance as other Claude use, while larger jobs require more turns. Inclusion in a subscription therefore does not mean unlimited unattended work. The vendor also acknowledges missed messages, misread threads and mistaken urgency. Compared with a reminder list, Duet can prepare material and proposed actions before the owner opens the app. The corresponding burden is understanding ongoing access, shared consumption and which decisions still need a person.
