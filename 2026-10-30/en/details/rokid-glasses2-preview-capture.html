Check the frame before the shutter

With glasses, the scene visible to the eyes is not necessarily the frame captured by the camera. Glasses 2 adds a preview before the shutter, letting the wearer check the boundary instead of discovering a clipped subject afterward. The official page also describes HDR, multi-frame low-light processing and video stabilization. Those are capability claims, not a measured everyday success rate. First-person capture can leave the hands involved in the activity, but composition remains an intentional action. Turning the head, tracking a moving subject and working in dim light are ordinary situations in which the relationship between preview and final result still needs to be experienced.

Meeting material includes sound and images

The launch description combines sound and first-person imagery for meeting records, with notes, summaries and action items as outputs. This can reduce the need to retype everything, but useful records still depend on capturing a readable whiteboard, attributing speech correctly and deciding which sentence constitutes a commitment. Immediate and day-long recall target remembered conversations and related events. Call translation also includes a voice-preserving option. Language coverage does not measure accuracy in every acoustic setting, and an extracted action item is not an executed action. The wearer must still check the person, date and intended meaning, particularly when the record becomes the basis for a later decision.

Speech capture and finger control solve different steps

The disclosed capture hardware includes four omnidirectional microphones and one bone-conduction microphone. Qualcomm and BES processors work with Android and RTOS to divide tasks, but naming those parts does not establish that every AI inference runs locally. For control, the wearer can use the temple, touch or voice, or add Rokid Ring for sliding, tapping, rotation and a configurable finger snap. Moving some controls away from the face may make certain interactions less conspicuous. It also adds a device to pair, charge and learn. The gesture descriptions do not disclose the complete sensing mechanism, so there is no basis for inventing an optical, muscle-sensing or other implementation.

Separate the models and their release stages

Glasses 2 retains a green display; the separately announced Rokid Color is a different full-color product. Its eye-tracking claim and weight must not be transferred to Glasses 2. October 27 dates this launch, while the October 29 English article is later reporting. Reports describe Chinese shipping planned for November 11 and an international debut at CES 2027, neither of which proves worldwide delivery today. The official battery capacity and external charging accessories indicate the intended endurance approach, but actual duration depends on capture, display and connected tasks. For sustained wear, optical clarity, nose pressure and usable records are more immediate tradeoffs than capacity alone, and remain untested here.
