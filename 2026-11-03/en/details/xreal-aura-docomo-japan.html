AURA adds a Japanese purchase and fitting route, with eye spacing part of the decision

The new information is the Japanese sales route

Global orders were announced on October 28, when Google also mentioned November 2 orders in Japan. DOCOMO now specifies its own route: online sales from November 27 and shops from December. Those are distinct channels, not evidence that every Japanese buyer could collect a unit on November 2. The distinction matters when comparing consultation, model selection and support. The announcement does not establish that a particular store already has a demonstration unit or a bookable fitting appointment, and Japanese pricing was not verified here. This item therefore covers the regional increment. Existing spatial-app capabilities provide context rather than being counted again as newly introduced features.

Eye spacing and the separate pack belong in the selection checklist

DOCOMO lists 12 GB/256 GB and 16 GB/512 GB models, each with M or L fitting. Its stated interpupillary ranges are 60–66mm and 66–72mm respectively. These details help identify unsuitable purchases more directly than a weight headline. The glasses figure excludes the separate wired compute pack, which handles processing and battery duties and can act as a trackpad. A user still needs somewhere to put it. Compare the setup with a monitor by arranging the cable, pack and any keyboard or mouse in the intended space. Comfort cannot be inferred solely from frame weight, nor can a listed eye-spacing range establish clear viewing for every individual.

Test visible content and usable controls separately

Existing capabilities include placing several apps in view, selecting with hands or the pack, and asking Gemini about viewed content. Google's wired-mirroring description adds a laptop screen beside Android XR apps through a video-capable USB-C connection. That could reduce the need to find an additional display while traveling. Seeing the mirrored content, however, does not establish equal assistant control over every desktop action. A meaningful demonstration would open a realistically sized document, check text and window edges, move focus and complete an edit. Watching an impressive large video exercises a different job. These proposed checks help connect the platform's mechanisms to work without inventing independent productivity measurements.

[S25]

Local sales do not promise identical features everywhere

DOCOMO qualifies functionality by country, region and language, with subscriptions potentially applying to some usage. Its illustrative screens also do not promise identical results. Buyers should verify the combination they need: account, language, applications and lens requirements. Price hardware, necessary accessories and ongoing services separately rather than borrowing a foreign-market starting figure. The inspected announcement does not show that shops can solve every fitting issue, and no independent long-session comfort data was collected here. A confirmed demonstration and clear return conditions would reduce more uncertainty than another comparison of headline specifications. Expanded distribution makes those checks more plausible, but does not itself prove they have been completed.
