AI interactions need to explain what happened

The design problem extends beyond arranging chat controls. People need to understand the information used, unresolved uncertainty and when they can take over. A portfolio could follow one failure or interruption: how the person notices it, preserves completed work and continues. That is an editorial suggestion based on the advertised work, not a published employer interview rubric. HouseSigma’s AI-company context should not be converted into an AI-skill requirement absent from its role.

A coded prototype and production delivery are different commitments

Calling all three “AI coding” hides different obligations. Ask who maintains code, handles failures and decides that delivery is complete. A working demo does not establish integration readiness, while strong research and component specifications do not imply release-duty ownership. Separate exploratory prototypes, handoff documentation and work actually shipped in a portfolio. Identify personal contributions and collaborators’ responsibilities rather than claiming the whole team’s result.

Outcome evidence should answer a specific question

Evidence should fit the task: what behavior was obstructed, which hypothesis was tested and what remained unresolved. Conversion is not the right measure for every role, and research-participant counts are not design benefits. A useful case connects constraints, choices and observations while retaining failed versions. Without a reliable before-and-after measure, describe the observation scope instead of inventing a savings percentage. Several roles ask for five or more years, while some use seniority labels without a number; this senior-skewed sample cannot establish industry-wide exclusion of juniors.

Confirm engagement terms before assuming schedule freedom

Part-time, remote and independent contracting describe different dimensions. Compare pay only after aligning the period, base salary versus contract fees, workload and self-funded costs; this list retains original currencies and does not annualize unknown hours. Eleven roles come from ten employers: Andgo’s two roles remain separate jobs but not independent employers. Six discoveries and five revalidations are not weekly hiring additions. With every original posting date unknown and no comparable historical cohort, this establishes a set of roles to revisit, not a hiring trend.
