HIGHLIGHT / H01

MorningAI: preview the change before publishing

Official announcement · not tested

2026-10-12

The dated update describes a GitHub-connected flow: select Website, request a change, inspect a plan and device previews, then choose Publish. This verifies the advertised workflow, not its success rate; no account or rollback was tested.

Editorial implication: the useful design unit is an inspectable state between requesting work and making it public. Test whether preview and deployed revision match, whether all changes are visible, and how concurrent edits are handled.

HIGHLIGHT / H05

Reliability research: harder users and replayable changes

Research signal · abstracts read / not replicated

2026-10-12

TIPER varies simulated user behaviour while preserving task solvability, reporting weaknesses missed by cooperative evaluations. agent-risk examines when logs can support a changed agent policy and when saved-state reruns are necessary. This issue checked abstracts and submission dates; it did not reproduce experiments or establish shipping capabilities.

Editorial implication: include hesitant users, changed instructions, incomplete information, and traceable policy changes in acceptance testing. Reuse a small task set across ordinary and varied behaviours, recording failure types rather than only an average score.

Deep Review: technical review becomes a dedicated workflow

Official public release · not tested

2026-10-14

Google publicly launched Deep Review, using multiple agents to examine technical documents and consolidate feedback. A dedicated upload-and-review surface makes checking more explicit than generic chat. No file was uploaded and accuracy was not tested. Editorial implication: assess whether each finding is traceable, dismissible, and correctable, rather than counting critiques. Check retention and deletion choices before sensitive uploads; a convenient review flow is not an automatically trustworthy verdict.

SUPPORT & COUNTEREVIDENCE

Hypothesis 1: inspectable intermediate states are becoming product surfaces

Hypothesis · baseline establishing

Support: site editing has previews, task execution exposes mode and progress, and spatial analysis exposes plans and sources. Developer event-correlation friction and replay research identify information needed for supervision. This suggests design convergence across products, not an adoption-rate trend.

Counterevidence and alternatives: launch copy may converge without effective implementation; users may ignore intermediate states; one issue cannot generalize to all frameworks. There is no comparable 4–8-week panel or payment evidence.
