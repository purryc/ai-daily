Timing and user: an announcement is not a household deployment

AiMOGA's October17 announcement, timestamped10:27Z, says Mobo will debut in Wuhu and open global preorders on October21. Relative to the original October20 cutoff at14:08Z, the announcement is three hours and forty-one minutes beyond the72-hour boundary. It belongs in the weekly lookback. The positioning is household companionship and everyday assistance, with voice, visual recognition and reminders among the promised capabilities. This review did not verify a retail price, delivery date, supported sales territories or independent hands-on report. Global preorders are not equivalent to delivery everywhere, and shipment figures for the company's other robots must not be attributed to Mobo.

Workflow: turn a household request into confirmable steps

A testable household job is reminding one person to bring event tickets before leaving on Saturday. The system must establish which person, which Saturday and what departure time, save the reminder, accept correction or cancellation, then trigger it in the agreed context. AiMOGA says MoAgent joins perception, interaction and execution through planning and memory; an EXEED release adds spatial and preference-related claims. These are product claims, not evidence that this complete reminder workflow is reliable. Product inference: household memory first needs inspectable ownership and correction. One resident's preference should not silently become a rule for everyone, and a canceled plan should stop influencing subsequent behavior.

Physical failure and adoption: unspecified controls must remain unspecified

A moving device adds physical conditions that a phone reminder does not: nearby people, obstacles and battery state. The releases do not specify physical mute, emergency stopping, fall recovery, offline operation or preservation of pending tasks under those conditions. A product photograph cannot supply missing controls, and a privacy slogan does not establish an auditable permission model. Companionship marketing should also not be converted into a childcare capability. Adoption questions include household-account management, deletion, connection-loss behavior, maintenance, service support, delivery and refund terms. None of those gaps was resolved by purchasing or registering a device. This issue has not tested Mobo's movement, voice performance or physical controls.

Alternatives and a falsifiable test: embodiment must earn its cost

Phone calendars, shared household lists and smart speakers are same-task alternatives. Mobo must show that movement or visual context improves the job rather than making a simple reminder harder to diagnose. A falsifiable evaluation would begin with ten stationary household tasks, deliberately adding rescheduling, residents with similar names, cancellation and a connection interruption. Measure correct triggering, false memory and recovery time before introducing movement. Natural conversation does not compensate for the wrong person or time. After October21, the valuable follow-up evidence would be an uninterrupted end-to-end demonstration, documented controls and concrete fulfillment terms. Additional feature names would not resolve those questions, and the announcement alone is insufficient for an adoption conclusion.
