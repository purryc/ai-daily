Keep the question beside the discussion

A technology purchasing discussion often sends one teammate to a research portal, after which they condense a report and paste it back into chat. The Slack app lets an eligible license holder bring the question into a channel, thread or direct message. Forrester says recent conversation context can inform the answer. This can help with terminology, comparisons and finding relevant material without repeatedly reconstructing the discussion. It does not mean the assistant knows the team’s budget, contracts or every earlier decision. Explicit assumptions remain useful: a passing suggestion in a thread should not silently become a settled requirement for the recommendation.

Separate shared answers from individual research

The marketplace separates individual research in Slack’s agents or apps area from answers shared into an existing conversation. Other participants can view the response and its supporting sources, including colleagues without a Forrester license. The shared object is therefore an answer the team can discuss together, rather than a private search result available only to its requester. Citations and related analyst resources provide a route for deeper investigation. Visibility of a summary should not be confused with unrestricted access to every report or capability. That distinction matters when a colleague needs to check a quotation or explore the evidence beyond the response.

[S38]

Use citations to inspect the recommendation

Forrester describes answers grounded exclusively in its own research and warns that generated information can be inaccurate. That is a defined body of expertise, not completed due diligence across every available market source. Start with the citation most relevant to the decision and inspect its date, geography, sample and qualifying conditions. A report comparing vendors may need a current product document beside it if the question concerns a recent feature. This use can reduce the effort of locating background material while preserving the researcher’s responsibility for scope and freshness. Fluent wording is not a reason to discard the source trail.

[S37]

Check account access and the sharing workflow

Access requires a license that includes Forrester AI, connecting the Forrester account and, potentially, Slack administrator approval. The release does not establish a universal public price, and this review did not measure response accuracy or time saved. A practical trial would use questions that normally require manual report searches, then record whether the answer found appropriate material, colleagues could follow its citations and important evidence still had to be added. If correcting stale interpretations consumes the search time saved, the integration has not improved that task. Existing portal search and manual sharing remain useful comparisons without assuming a convenient chat entry produces better business decisions.
