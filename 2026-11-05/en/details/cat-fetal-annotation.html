Remove the handoff between viewing and transcription

Preparing imaging data requires both interpreting the image and recording exactly which frames receive a label. CAT combines playback, navigation and keyboard labeling in a local window, then exports structured annotations. It addresses a clerical handoff that can consume attention beside the specialist task. A product team should keep these two contributions distinct. Reducing typing and window switching can be valuable even if experts still disagree about an image. Conversely, a cleaner export does not establish diagnostic correctness. The appropriate user is a trained annotator preparing research data, rather than a patient seeking an automated interpretation.

Constrain data entry with change points

A label is stored at a change point and persists until the next change or the end of the file. This prevents conflicting intervals and out-of-range entries by construction. The initial default is non-target, however, so absence of a blank range is not evidence that someone reviewed every frame. An adoption check should distinguish reviewed non-target material from material that simply retained its default. It should also verify that exported labels still identify the correct source file. Structural consistency is an important property, but a workflow needs a separate account of review coverage and unresolved interpretation.

Do not extrapolate student efficiency to experts

The study combines an audit of twenty-five examinations with a randomized crossover involving six medical students using simplified scans. Correctly labeled frames per minute were about 1.68 times the spreadsheet result, without a detectable accuracy difference. The expert comparison used partly different annotators months apart, lacked an independent adjudicated reference and did not measure time. Those details limit what a buyer can infer. More segments may mean finer annotation rather than better boundaries. A local comparison should therefore measure the relevant professionals on the same material and separate typing errors, interpretation disagreements and actual time saved.

Training labels still need calibration

The paper describes bundled macOS and Windows execution using Python and Tkinter. Predictive machine-learning assistance and a study dashboard are future extensions. A pilot could include both straightforward and difficult imaging, checking quality-grade agreement, export completeness and rework. It should examine propagation errors: an incorrect change point may affect an entire following segment. Keyboard efficiency can magnify a mistake as well as remove repetitive entry. If a cleaner structure still contains overlooked defaults or inconsistent quality judgments, the remaining work is calibration and review. The study does not show that every exported dataset is automatically ready for training or clinical validation.
