Keep the material beside the keyboard

A progress reply often starts with work that happens before typing: gathering an engineering update, a production schedule and the facts a manager actually needs. iFlytek 16.0 accepts supplied material and a description of the intended message, then returns a draft to the current text field. The sender retains the final send action. This is useful when the obstacle is organizing known facts into a short explanation on a phone. It is less obviously a substitute for a document editor when the work requires detailed citations, tracked revisions or complex formatting. The proposed convenience is keeping preparation close to the place where the message will be used, rather than opening another assistant and carrying its answer back.

Dictation and drafting remain different jobs

The developer listing distinguishes several controls: an AI handoff key, voice input from the space bar, optional AI improvement and spoken commands that revise an utterance. Keeping those jobs distinct matters. Someone recording an exact statement wants faithful text; someone asking for a customer reply wants help organizing an intention. A fluent rewrite is not a reliable way to check names, amounts, negation or deadlines. The launch material identifies Spark-ASR-2.0 as the speech-recognition component, but no independent noisy-room or dialect test was performed here. Users should choose the operation that matches the task rather than treating every polished result as a more accurate transcription.

Reduce repeated setup without assuming shared context

Task context and personal preference serve different purposes. One establishes the material for the current message; the other guides how the person tends to express it. Configurable skill groups can keep recurring writing actions close at hand. The practical tradeoff is preparation: users still have to select relevant material and notice when a copied update is stale. A keyboard receiving three fragments does not thereby know the whole project. Compared with a saved prompt in a separate chat application, the keyboard may remove a transfer step, but it also places more responsibility on a small interaction surface. The available descriptions do not establish a complete interface for inspecting, correcting and deleting remembered preferences.

Choose decoration separately from writing assistance

The new keyboard companion reacts to typing and offers animated interaction and cosmetic progression. That is a separate personalization choice, not evidence that the writing engine is more accurate. A person who enjoys a visible character and someone who needs an unobtrusive work surface may value it differently. Xiaomi App Store dates 16.0.1 to October 22, so this issue treats it as a recent supplement rather than a new October 23 release. The download is listed as free; exact AI charges, quotas and availability on other platforms were not verified. The immediate adoption question is concrete: whether the keyboard reduces gathering and transferring material in the messaging apps a person actually uses, while leaving the resulting message easy to inspect and change.
