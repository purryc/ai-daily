Getting the first visit onto a phone

Birdbuddy addresses a small, recognizable job: see the bird that just visited without continuously watching the window, then learn what it is. With Nook, the owner inserts the camera module, creates an account and pairs it in the app before hanging or mounting the feeder. Marks around the circular module show portrait and landscape orientation; rotating the camera changes the composition at capture rather than relying only on a later crop. Visits trigger recording, and the app provides photos, video and a live view. Identification connects those images to species information, reducing the separate task of comparing each visitor with a field guide.

Two sizes, different maintenance routines

Nook holds roughly 0.9 litres of seed and costs $99; the $159 solar option adds a detachable roof panel that can face either direction. The $219 Home holds about 1.4 litres, integrates two solar panels and provides a larger perch and a speaker. Connectivity is another meaningful distinction: Home supports both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks, while Nook needs 2.4 GHz. A household running only 5 GHz must address its network configuration rather than expecting the app to compensate. Greater capacity can reduce refilling, but placement still depends on available space, sunlight, bird activity and actual wireless coverage. Solar assistance does not eliminate charging in every season or garden.

Manual and automatic identification are different entitlements

The purchase page separates ownership from automation. Basic use includes photos, videos, livestreaming and user-initiated bird identification. Automatically identifying visitors, naming regulars and tracking their visits belong to Premium. The family offer observed here provides one trial month and then bills $98.99 annually, with Premium for ten guests; that should not be conflated with an individual plan quoted in media coverage. Someone occasionally curious about an unfamiliar bird may be satisfied by a manual identification step. An owner expecting an automatically organized record of recurring visitors needs to include renewal costs. Sharing the feeder experience and sharing paid entitlements are related but distinct choices.

Ordering, delivery and everyday care

The October 22 report marks the order launch, while the designs had appeared earlier as Birdbuddy2 Mini and Birdbuddy2. The current buying pages explicitly say deliveries begin in November. Assembly and care instructions also reveal work omitted from a cheerful garden photograph: Nook’s roof opens for refilling, its front insert must return to the correct slots, and electronics must be removed before washing the seed compartment. Documentation deserves a further check before purchase: the Nook specification table says Full HD, while a generic FAQ mentions 2K Nature Cam Pro capture. The exact module and subscription mode should resolve that inconsistency; it is not evidence that every advertised resolution is included in every configuration.
