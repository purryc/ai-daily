Eight lanes: scans, exclusions and gaps

official · Original sources and supporting documentation; bounded sample

The official lane is bounded by the transferred candidate list. Validation revisited announcements, support pages, releases and author sites, keeping collection time separate from availability. Mintlify and Jotform provide October 20 calendar dates without a decisive main-window clock, so they remain supplements. Earlier Jotform guides also prevent calling the announcement first availability. Packr's original explicitly dates Windows general availability to October 19; later distribution does not reset that date. ServiceNow's Arc GA statement, planned Windows version and future AI Passport retain separate delivery labels. Saviynt's private preview does not inherit the existing Zuma free-trial entitlement. NVIDIA, ASUS and Sereact combine hardware, software and integration announcements, requiring component-specific status rather than treating everything in a release as newly shipped. Some direct page opens failed and were checked through accessible original-page content. Those failures are access limitations, not evidence of absence. Account-specific entitlements, actual enforcement, recovery and physical performance remain follow-up work. This issue did not operate paid accounts or perform field acceptance tests.

reviews · Independent hands-on coverage incomplete

This review lane did not obtain independent hands-on material sufficient to reproduce the principal performance claims. The Maxwell case retains the vendor's two-B200, five-second 4K workload and its elapsed-time comparison; bit-identical audio is kept separate from the video similarity measurement. KINO-2's simulated picking result, action-chunk latency and order completion answer different questions. Sereact's robot-arm shipment count cannot become a count of customer sites. Regional exhibition photography can establish the presented appearance, but not continuous operation through a production shift. The inspected HD Hyundai photograph carries explicit usage restrictions and was not treated as cleared merely because its pixels were visible. Vendor demonstrations, issuer releases and syndicated reporting remain within their respective origin groups instead of being counted as independent confirmations. The missing evidence concerns representative tasks, equipment configuration, failure distributions and continuing maintenance cost. A useful follow-up is a controlled comparison under the same acceptance criteria, including interventions and recovery. Demonstration speed, reservation totals and polished imagery cannot substitute for that evidence, and this gap is not a claim that no independent reviewer exists.

community · Specific maintainer threads; broad user sampling incomplete

The community lane retains specific failures and maintainer responses rather than treating a public repository as a satisfaction survey. Historical OpenClaw issues distinguish stale tool discovery after a Gateway restart from completion messages lost around configuration reloads. Starting another session is a workaround, and this candidate remains outside the accepted set because its interrupted image operation was unresolved. Pydantic AI's merged discussion makes the difference between completed generation and completed playback inspectable; previously obtained history snapshots do not update retroactively. This is implementation evidence from maintainers, not an independently rerun failure rate. Copilot CLI's stable release and two previews retain their own timestamps and behavior. Skipping an unauthenticated MCP connection does not complete the step depending on it. No representative new sample of Reddit, Hacker News, V2EX or general public comments was established. Discussion volume is not evidence of willingness to pay. Follow-up should connect repeated reports to configuration, the actual repair version and external side effects. A single release note cannot support a blanket reliability conclusion across every user environment.

wild · Startup and prototype originals; discovery/comment coverage incomplete

Startup and unconventional-entry validation followed candidate originals for Lovart, Arda, RoboParty, Mobo, GripSound and public code, preserving differences in maturity. Lovart's October 19 announcement was collected after this issue's cutoff and is explicitly a delayed discovery. Its signed-out interface does not establish successful paid scheduling. Arda is a public unveiling with earlier deployment context, not its first customer installation. RoboParty concerns a Southeast Asian route rather than another global debut after September. Mobo's preorder state was verified only after cutoff, while GripSound's older release gave a scheduled launch date. Neither later observation can backfill a pre-cutoff availability fact. The researchers reported incomplete Product Hunt and comment coverage, with old or duplicate DIY leads. No additional accepted DIY item was invented to fill the category. ClicData MCP was investigated but had earlier material describing the same functionality and was not promoted as fresh news. Useful follow-up should obtain reproducible materials, delivery terms and task evidence. Rankings, waitlists and deposits do not establish retention or successful use.

research · Original papers, histories and reported experiments

Research validation checked original arXiv abstracts and version histories, separating submission timestamps from unconfirmed public-announcement times. Idealworks' two fleet papers form one project, but memory question answering and navigation experiments are not combined into a delivered autonomous loop. LiveUIBench evaluates state in AppWorld services rather than real customer accounts. CanvasObs improves target selection more than full task completion, so the most favorable intermediate measure is not substituted for the whole editing job. ValiAssist, FENCE, Sidecar and Rehearsim examine rule generalization, memory admission, thinking during waits and interview rehearsal. Sample limitations, mental workload, absence of a control group and synthetic-participant risks remain visible. REACT's author demonstrations do not establish everyday physical safety. Earlier counts for PhonoBridge and PointHand included their summaries; their separate deep reads fall below the retained gate and their image checks remain unresolved. Paper figures, actual interfaces, generated illustrations and software licenses are recorded separately. A public repository does not establish a complete training release or unrestricted commercial rights. No paper was independently replicated in this issue.

patent · Provider/access gap; no patent promoted

The patent lane remains an explicit access gap. The transferred record reports that the existing patent provider was unavailable. It did not supply a current, verifiable combination of publication text, publication date, applicant, family, claims and drawings. This validation created no account, credential or paid search channel, and it did not reclassify a product architecture as patent evidence. Mitsubishi's task library, NVIDIA's secure workspace and robot vendors' development routes may warrant later investigation, but their current sources remain announcements, documentation or papers. The gap does not prove that relevant applications do not exist, establish exclusive rights, or demonstrate a production plan. A subsequent check must first identify the public text and family, separating priority, submission, publication, grant and expiry dates. Relevant claims should then be connected to the actual interaction mechanism. Leads that fail those checks remain observations outside project and independent-event counts. Counting filings would not establish maturity, adoption or revenue. This edition therefore discloses the access limitation instead of supplying an invented patent story to complete a category.

china · Regional originals; limited independent evidence

China and adjacent Asian leads include RoboParty's regional research platform, AiMOGA's household reservation funnel, GripSound's wearable speaker, and manufacturing prototypes announced in Japan and Korea. Regional tags do not add independent sources. RoboParty distinguishes the educational DIY-oriented RPO from the research-oriented RP1, while complete stack licensing and reproduction remain unaudited. Mobo's ninety-nine-dollar deposit creates a three-hundred-ninety-nine-dollar purchase credit; neither number is the robot's total price, and shipment evidence is absent. GripSound's noise suppression must not be rewritten as a general-purpose agent assistant. HD Hyundai's force sensing, mobility and walking directions retain future status, and the photograph's usage restrictions affect visual acceptance. Mitsubishi's source clock lacks a timezone, which is not supplied from geographical assumptions. This issue did not establish a Chinese-language independent hands-on sample or verified buyer transactions. Further checks should address regional delivery, repairs, language interaction and account entitlement. A global announcement heading cannot establish identical access across markets or serve as evidence that regional support is ready.

global · Cross-region comparison within the recovered sample

Global comparison is organized around shared tasks rather than company prominence. Enterprise execution connects Arc's continuing follow-up, Zuma's action governance and OpenShell's deployment boundaries. Manufacturing compares Sereact's returns station, Arda's propagation of design changes and research robot platforms. Private preview, general availability, reference architecture, early access and a future pilot remain distinct delivery forms. Gemini's government package targets United States federal, state and local institutions rather than worldwide governments. ASUS configurations and support still depend on regional contracts. Existing operation at European sites is separate from today's partnership network or training-architecture announcement. Original releases and their distribution channels belong to one origin group; more domains do not create independent confirmation. Historical comparison uses readable source ledgers and published indexes from the preceding eight weeks, without claiming inaccessible history was fully checked. This lane supports bounded supply and interaction signals for the present issue. It lacks continuous user, revenue and retention data needed for a market-growth conclusion. Subsequent comparisons should keep task conditions and commercial availability consistent across regions.

Held and excluded: specific gaps

OpenClaw, PhonoBridge and PointHand lack completed permitted image checks; the latter two also fall below the per-project deep-body threshold. HD Hyundai’s substantive photograph carries reuse restrictions without established permission. Arda’s three candidates show generic machinery or CAD rather than an accepted product interface. GripSound’s original announcement lies outside seven days, and planned sales do not prove pre-cutoff availability. Mobo’s preorder state was verified only after cutoff; earlier capabilities were covered yesterday. Mitsubishi’s release lacks an explicit timezone, leaving cutoff ordering unresolved. The inventory contains 41 candidates; 33 pass this issue’s content and visual selection.
