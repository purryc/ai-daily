01 · Task and evidence

Lovart’s public announcement is dated October 19, 2026, and the vendor video displays the same publication date. This issue collected the evidence on October 21, so Arti is a delayed-discovery supplement from the preceding three days, not today’s launch. Lovart says version 2.0 is available on its website and positions Arti as an added layer within the existing design agent. The relevant job extends beyond making an occasional image: designers and marketers repeatedly gather references, explain brand preferences, produce asset families and reconcile feedback. A small business owner may both operate and pay for the tool; an agency must also distinguish the designer, client approver and purchaser. Those roles describe a plausible workflow, not verified adoption or willingness to pay.

02 · Workflow and handoffs

Previously, a user could restate preferences in each project, collect references elsewhere, return to the canvas for batch generation, inspect individual outputs and transfer comments between tools. Lovart’s own before-and-after comparison places the update at those handoffs: memory persists between projects, background research produces a sourced report, commissions recur on a schedule, and feedback attaches to particular elements. The workflow begins in Arti or Design delegation. Users describe the deliverable and constraints, attach references, select the destination project, and configure a one-time, daily, weekly or monthly run with a credit ceiling. A trial precedes committing the task. Results arrive on the project canvas for further editing, while history and actual consumption support review. Reduced coordination is a value hypothesis; this issue has not measured time savings.

03 · Control and recovery

Control deserves more scrutiny than the promise of autonomy. The assistant documentation describes inspectable, editable and deletable memory, with a choice over cross-project use. That matters when one designer serves several brands: a preference learned for one client should not silently become another client’s requirement. Design Review proposes issues and asks before applying fixes. Comments become a revision plan that the user can approve or change. People still need to judge whether suggestions follow the brief, references support their claims and reviewers contradict one another. The basic documentation says edited versions become new assets while originals remain, providing an asset-level recovery route. That does not establish whole-run rollback, automatic retry, recovery from partial completion or credit reimbursement. No generation, delegation or recovery test was performed here.

04 · Adoption and costs

Adoption costs require careful billing interpretation. The annual-billing interface observed on October 21 displayed monthly equivalents of $16, $27, $45 and $99 for Starter, Basic, Pro and Ultimate. The latter two were promotional, with next-year renewal equivalents of $74 and $166 per month, still billed annually. The page used a dollar symbol; an ISO currency was not confirmed through checkout. These are not independently verified monthly-subscription quotes. Daily delegated-task allowances were respectively two, four, eight and ten, with equal checkup allowances and one, two, four and five research runs. Although a limited Free plan was visible, its Arti eligibility was unconfirmed. Consumption varies with models, settings and complexity, and monthly credits do not roll over. Evaluate cost per accepted deliverable, including revisions, rather than treating generation estimates as usable production capacity.

05 · Evidence and scope

Manual alternatives remain a calendar, reusable briefs, reference boards, design templates and human review, with coordination spread across tools. Template editors and scripted batch processing are relevant competing approaches for the same task. If the job is only deterministic resizing or export, configuring an agent and maintaining memory may add unnecessary work. Arti has a stronger hypothesis where one brand repeatedly needs research, variants and feedback reconciliation. A small comparison using a representative campaign should record accepted assets, manual corrections, missed constraints, credit spend and recovery effort against the existing workflow. Reject the migration hypothesis if checking mistakes and maintaining memory absorb the coordination savings. The signed-out Arti page exposed feature controls but returned a request failure in its memory area. That limits this observation; it neither demonstrates authenticated product failure nor verifies working functionality.
