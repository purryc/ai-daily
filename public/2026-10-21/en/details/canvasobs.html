01 · Workflow and handoffs

Visual proximity on a canvas does not necessarily imply structural connection. A shape that appears inside a frame may not belong to it, and an arrow touching another object may not be bound to it. A person can inspect selection state, layers and drag feedback, while an agent reading only a screenshot may confuse appearance with document semantics. CanvasObs provides structured observations in tldraw covering identity, editability, visibility, containment, bindings and off-screen objects. The previous workflow relies on interpreting an image, guessing a target and correcting mistakes after acting. The proposed workflow first establishes which objects and relationships an action can affect. This improves the observation interface; it does not replace the editor’s action execution or undo mechanisms.

02 · Evidence and scope

The experiment holds the model, action set and executor fixed within its comparisons. Ten configurations, twenty repetitions, three models and two conditions produce 1, 200 trials and 600 paired comparisons. The authors report target-selection success increasing from 16.5 to 48.3 percent, while full task completion rises from 4.3 to 25.0 percent. That gap matters for product design: knowing which object to edit does not guarantee a correct movement, binding or final arrangement. For one model, the interval for full-task improvement includes zero, so the findings cannot be represented as established improvement for every model. The study also does not isolate a general advantage over every form of native-document access. Before adopting a new observation layer, a team should determine how much equivalent information its existing editor API already exposes and which failure modes remain after the target is identified.

03 · Acceptance and alternatives

The integration cost lies in maintaining semantics as the document changes. If an object is locked, moved off-screen, regrouped or unbound, the structured view must change accordingly. A once-correct but stale observation can cause an incorrect action just as an ambiguous screenshot can. Manual alternatives include naming layers, locking protected objects, checking connections individually and combining screenshots with native document data.

04 · Acceptance and alternatives

Acceptance can be split into target selection, edit outcome, relationship preservation and undo. Test visually similar objects, off-screen targets and cases where apparent containment differs from actual containment. The falsifiable product judgment is not whether the agent can describe the canvas persuasively, but whether it reduces damaging edits while preserving recovery. If better targeting still leads to broken connections, attempts to alter protected objects or edits that cannot be reversed, the observation improvement does not yet justify broader unattended execution. Its value should be measured against the full editing job rather than the most improved intermediate metric.
