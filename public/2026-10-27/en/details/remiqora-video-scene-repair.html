The work between individual generated clips

A usable generated clip leaves considerable work before it becomes a short film: writing the next prompt, keeping the character recognizable, choosing a continuation frame and joining sound and transitions. Remiqora Video keeps these operations inside one local project. The author explicitly describes it as a layer around existing models, not a newly trained video model. A local language model turns an idea into editable scene cards. Repeated character descriptions help maintain continuity, and a creator can pin satisfactory scenes while asking for other passages to be rewritten. The reduced effort is moving material between generation and editing tools; deciding whether the story and pacing work remains a creative task.

A changed shot has a visible dependency cost

A continuation uses the final decoded frame of the preceding scene as its starting image. That also explains why changing one shot can require more than one replacement: subsequent continuations depend on it. The cache documentation keys scenes by their prompts, seed, dimensions, length, model, LoRA and reference material. Unchanged scenes can be reused. Changing the character’s appearance or the model across the film invalidates more work, whereas ordinary soundtrack or output changes mainly require mixing and processing. For someone trying alternative endings, this dependency boundary is useful information. It explains the cost of a revision rather than promising that every small edit will be cheap.

[S05]

Automatic repair retains the original take

When an action looks wrong, the scene inspector can request another seed or change only that scene’s action and camera instructions. Earlier takes remain selectable. Separate checks examine prompt constraints before generation and signals such as black frames, frozen footage or jumps afterward. The author describes code-based measurements and small vision models, rather than a language model acting as a film critic. Severe detected problems receive one automatic replacement take, with both retained; other warnings identify a time to inspect. This can direct attention toward a suspect second. It cannot replace watching the acting, meaning and sound of the finished sequence.

[S06] [S07]

The editor handoff has specific limits

The montage provides trimming, speed, transitions, audio and color before either building a movie or handing FCPXML and media to DaVinci Resolve. The export guide specifies what travels: trims, speed, dissolves and levels do; automatic music ducking and video color do not. A flash becomes a marker, and an ungenerated scene leaves a gap. These details tell the next editor what still needs attention. The first version requires Windows 11 and an RTX GPU with 16 GB of memory, with roughly 53 GB for the minimum model set. The project uses GPL-3.0 while models retain separate licenses. It remains an early release with an unsigned installer, and the README acknowledges face drift in long continuations.
