Extracting calibration evidence from activity

Inserting a key, connecting a cable or marking paper can provide precise visual targets. GazeAgent proposes events from IMU, gaze and hand signals, then uses a VLM to validate relevance and locate a target region. Accepted correspondences update calibration. Research uses Project Aria, a SocialEye baseline and fine-tuned Qwen3.5-35B-A3B. It maintains measurement quality rather than authorizing actions from a glance.

Denominators and compute boundaries

The scripted study involved ten people and 820 predefined opportunities. Initial proposal recall was complete; semantic validation achieved 95.9% recall and 92.3% precision. Under disturbance, median gaze error fell 44.1% versus explicit calibration; a separate case study involved five people. Current inference is offline: approximately 24 GB for the quantized model, with resident-weight median calls of 9.4 seconds for validation and 2.4 seconds for localization. Optional feedback can confirm, reject or correct targets. This does not establish real-time operation on glasses.

Product analysis: manage stale calibration before promising invisibility

PM analysis: the strongest initial setting is a work period with frequent glasses movement and clear hand-eye tasks, rather than continuous selection of tiny buttons. A downstream interface should know the age, usable range and uncertainty of its calibration. When evidence stops arriving, target selection should seek additional confirmation instead of silently treating old parameters as reliable. Conversely, asking about every uncertain candidate could turn background maintenance into another interruption-heavy calibration procedure. A useful interface would collect low-confidence cases for a natural pause and provide a clear route back to explicit calibration. Product teams should also separate maintaining a useful estimate from proving that one particular selection is correct. Gaze can nominate an object while another input confirms consequential actions. These state and takeover proposals are editorial product analysis, not established guarantees of the research prototype.

Alternatives and failure testing

The practical baselines are explicit recalibration when needed and a larger gaze target that tolerates error. One costs interruption; the other costs interface density. A trial should compare whole work sessions, including periods without useful events. I would include prolonged reading, hidden hands, removing and replacing the glasses, and looking at one object while touching another. Record actual minutes until acceptable accuracy returns, degradation caused by bad pseudo-targets, feedback requests, energy use and main-task success. Introduce incorrect correspondences at increasing rates and evaluate on separate targets to discover when updating should stop. The hypothesis weakens if improvement needs continual correction or recovery takes longer than an explicit procedure. Procurement should therefore begin as a research integration with a fallback, rather than assume a finished consumer feature. This review verified the paper, not an installable product or a code license for the complete system.
