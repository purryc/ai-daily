Keep the support handoff in one conversation

Desk365’s earlier Teams bots already created and tracked tickets. The October 31 release adds an AI entry point that starts with a question in ordinary language. An employee explains the problem, receives an answer based on selected support content and, when that is insufficient, answers follow-up questions before reviewing a drafted ticket. After submission, assignments, status changes and human replies return to the same conversation, as do approval requests and satisfaction surveys. This removes some copying between chat and a helpdesk portal. It does not establish that AI resolves every difficult issue: escalated work still goes to the support team, while the employee keeps a visible route to follow progress.

Collect the fields the team actually needs

AI ticket forms are configured separately for the support portal, website chat and Teams. Each channel has a default, and administrators can add specialized alternatives. The agent uses their names and descriptions to select a likely match and fills the subject and description. The contact can change the form through a dropdown and edit its contents before submission. Custom fields and attachments help a repair or access request carry the information a human team needs, reducing another round of questions. Form selection remains a model judgment rather than guaranteed business validation. Ambiguous descriptions can produce a poor match, which makes the visible correction control useful instead of hiding the routing decision from the requester.

Configure access and the knowledge boundary

Before deployment, an administrator lists permitted email domains in helpdesk settings. Only matching contacts can use the Teams agent; when several helpdesks share a domain, the helpdesk subdomain directs the request to the right destination. An organization can deploy and pin the app centrally, but a Teams administrator may need to allow it where third-party apps are restricted. Plus or Premium and enabled AI are prerequisites. Knowledge access is also configured rather than universal: an operator chooses knowledge-base categories, pages, files or question-and-answer material, assigns sources to an agent and tests responses. Channel deployment and knowledge maintenance remain separate responsibilities, including checking synchronization after source material changes.

Do not equate confidence with resolution

The dashboard reports messages, escalations and resolution, but its guide defines a resolved message through a confidence score above0.4. Its resolution rate also reflects conversations handled without escalation. These measures can identify conversations worth examining; they do not independently prove that an employee’s problem was solved. Operators can inspect recent conversation history, credit consumption and the associated contact, then compare answers with actual ticket outcomes. The accompanying update also records changes to automation rules and API-driven ticket actions in audit logs. Knowing who changed a rule or sent a response is different from establishing correctness. The workflow’s practical value therefore depends on maintained knowledge and an effective human handoff, alongside the conversational interface.
