Keep the parts that already work

An ad revision often concerns one small detail: shorten a headline, change a button color or move a logo. A prompt-only correction can disturb a composition that was already approved. Hera’s creative canvas accepts brand material, references and a brief, then keeps alternatives together for comparison. Its official example separates a background, glow, logo, headline, card and button. This gives the creator concrete objects to inspect and revise. A practical first task is an existing campaign with one known correction. Compare what survives that edit, including spacing and alignment, rather than judging only whether a newly generated draft looks attractive at first glance.

Separate layer editing from motion control

The maker distinguishes static editing from animation control. Text, logos and shapes can be adjusted directly, while manual control over motion is still limited. That matters when a client approves the layout but requests a particular entrance, pause or transition. A layered design should not be assumed to offer the same timing control as a dedicated timeline editor. Review the beginning, middle and end after animating an approved composition. If a word change alters pacing or causes a late overlap, the remaining work belongs in the estimate. The published capabilities support a more editable draft, but do not establish complete replacement of a motion designer’s finishing tools.

[S05]

A shared board supports comparison, not campaign judgment

The website presents parallel agents and shared brand material as a way to explore alternatives in one place. One direction might emphasize product detail and another its use in context. Keeping them together can reduce repeated downloading and assembling a comparison board. It can also create more near-identical options to review. Set the required wording, format and protected brand elements before asking for variations, and record why a version was selected. Visual preference and campaign performance answer different questions. Nothing in the inspected material establishes automatic selection of the best-performing ad, and a larger collection of designs is not evidence of higher sales or stronger audience response.

Treat this as a canvas update and check the working costs

Product Hunt dates this presentation November 2 and separately records the earlier April launch-video product. The exact availability time was not established, so this is a supplemental update rather than a precise first-release claim. Hera’s terms permit personal and commercial use while leaving responsibility for supplied assets with the user. They also describe subscriptions and credits; one free manual correction does not make the whole workflow free. A complete current tariff was not verified. For a trial, record generation time, correction effort and export review separately. Include two client briefs to check that similar logos, wording or visual directions do not become confused during repeated revisions.
