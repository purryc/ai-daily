Redact what the recording already contains

App demos often start with real interactions, leaving an email, phone number or key briefly visible. TapCraft 2.5.1, dated October 30, adds on-device sensitive-information finding with review and deselection before masks are applied. A mask can follow scrolling text, reducing repeated repositioning for the same object. The notes also describe fixing text that could remain faintly visible through a blurred mask inside a magnifier, where masks now render solid. This affects what viewers actually see, so a thumbnail preview is insufficient acceptance. No independent frame-by-frame export test was performed, and the feature should not be presented as complete protection against disclosure.

Tap positions have an explicit source

The core workflow imports an Android or iOS recording, arranges it in a phone model and adds tap ripples, zooms, captions or sound. The FAQ explicitly says tap coordinates come from manual placement on the preview or an optional adb capture tool. An automatic detector was removed after producing too many false positives. Camera movement following taps therefore does not establish understanding of the app’s actions. A creator can reserve a magnifier for one price or toggle and use a broader camera movement for an actual interaction. Whether the result communicates clearly still depends on pacing and explanation; more animated effects do not by themselves produce a better demonstration.

[S15]

Review AI copy separately from store assets

The existing Store assets feature combines screenshots, device frames and editable headlines into multiple output sizes delivered in a zip. AI can draft a headline for each screen, but that does not validate the app’s promises. The convenience lies in avoiding repeated resizing and recreating the same promotional layout. A device-framed promotional video also differs from an App Store App Preview; TapCraft’s own FAQ cautions that the framed format may not be accepted in that slot. Start with one real interaction, check that the copy describes what the screen actually does, then inspect cropping, text and destination separately rather than treating the export package as universally suitable.

Local computation and stored uploads differ

The free tier allows three watermarked video exports. Pro includes sensitive-information finding and additional production features; the applicable checkout should determine price rather than an older FAQ or a temporary promotion. The current notes describe local detection and the website describes browser rendering, while the August privacy page describes cloud storage of uploaded media and projects, with older rendering wording. Local scanning therefore cannot be generalized into a claim that the recording never leaves the device. Project deletion should also be considered alongside the uploads library. A non-sensitive sample is a useful first check of storage, masking and download behavior before real client footage is introduced.
