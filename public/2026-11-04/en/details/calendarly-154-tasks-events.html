Calendarly brings Todoist tasks into event layouts and adds Live Activity filters

Tasks join appointments in the same view

The latest version can place Todoist tasks among calendar events in layouts and widgets through Show with events. Someone who schedules appointments in a calendar but tracks work separately gains a shared glanceable view. The Tasks Live Activity also gains controls for dated or undated reminders and for all lists or a particular list. These filters matter because a narrow display can otherwise mix unrelated commitments. The App Store lists version 1.5.4 as updated fourteen hours before observation. That provides the fresh event for this record; the underlying wallpaper app existed much earlier and should not be presented as a new product launch.

Wallpaper refresh is an explicit automation

The setup guide starts inside the app, requests calendar access and adds a wallpaper-update shortcut that runs once. The user then creates a daily Time of Day automation in Shortcuts. Immediate execution prevents it from waiting behind a notification. The artifact is a newly drawn image, visible when the phone returns to its Lock Screen. The guide specifies a photo Lock Screen because self-rendering styles such as Weather or Astronomy cannot accept the replacement image. This gives the no-app-opening promise a concrete dependency chain. Permission refusal, a missing shortcut or an incorrectly configured automation can leave the old image in place.

System calendar access precedes direct integrations

The current path begins with calendars and reminder lists already available on the phone. In a launch reply, the maker explains that a Google calendar synchronized into Apple Calendar can already appear, while a more direct integration remains planned. A promised release week is not evidence of completed integration. The official gallery shows day plans, weekly grids, countdowns and notes as alternative treatments of the same personal information. The design question is how much belongs in one glance. A wallpaper can reduce distracting navigation, but packing a week of work, miscellaneous tasks and long notes together may defeat that benefit. Layout variety alone does not establish better follow-through.

Local display and external sync have separate boundaries

The official site states that no Calendarly account is required and lists iOS 16.6 or later. Its free selection covers96 layouts, with Pro adding further designs, personal photos and combined arrangements. The store distinguishes locally drawn calendar and reminder information from the optional Todoist connection; calendar wallpaper works offline after setup, while task synchronization and purchases need connectivity. The release also claims more frequent widget updates when the app has not been opened, without giving an interval or promising immediate delivery of every change. A useful device check would follow one modified task to each display surface and measure when it appears, rather than judging freshness from an attractive example image.
