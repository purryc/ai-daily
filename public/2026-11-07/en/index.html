PHIL helps robot trainers inspect predicted end-effector motion aligned with the physical workspace through a Quest 3 interface.

Holding the controller trigger takes over; releasing it returns control. The policy keeps predicting during takeover, but those shadow actions do not execute. Prediction and execution must remain distinguishable; spatial calibration and takeover latency affect judgment, and releasing the trigger transfers control back immediately.

Rejected predictions and executed corrections from the same state and horizon become preference pairs for post-training.

Each condition uses five correction episodes and twenty-five evaluation rollouts: four tasks for Diffusion Policy and three for X-VLA, not a participant study.

Product perspective

Preview interpretation and training benefit need separate checks. The research does not establish novice usability or safety certification; this is a seven-day retrospective.

ProGram updates charts during computation and keeps linked views in step

ProGram targets interactive analytics developers, using declarative queries to schedule incremental computation and stream intermediate results into a web interface.

Users can pause, resume or prioritize queries and derive a new chart from existing intermediate state. Clustering and projection views can advance over shared chunks. The interface must show coverage and stability so users can decide whether to wait longer or revise their question.

Two reproduced analytics systems and eleven examples demonstrate expressiveness, not measured improvements in user decisions.

The backend currently accepts CSV, and some filter changes require restarting computation. Code is promised after acceptance rather than verified available now.

Product perspective

Interfaces still need to make incompleteness visible so a changing chart is not mistaken for a final answer. This is a recent research supplement.

AI feedback changes tutor prompts, but not talk balance

The study turns tutoring recordings into post-session statistics, transcript excerpts and specific suggestions for the tutor’s next session.

Tutors review feedback before their next session. Classifiers detect discourse moves and GPT-4 Turbo generates suggestions; this is not a new model-product launch. A tutor’s specific questioning behavior and the group’s talk share are different targets; placeholder figure values are not participant observations.

The paper was submitted November 4 and reports two 2024 bootcamps, making it a recent research supplement rather than newly deployed software.

It involves 1,030 distinct tutors and 7,443 learners, with a smaller complete-case regression sample than the overall recorded-session set.

Product perspective

Measures of eliciting ideas, uptake and reasoning improve, but talk balance does not. Subsequent SAT-score improvement was not established.
