Mop geometry changes contact, not the clutter in a room

The R2 uses curved triangular pads with offset motion, including an extending assembly intended to reach closer to edges. Dyson describes separate mechanical collection of larger debris and suction for fine dust, with dirt and floor sensing informing operation. Users supply room choices, schedules and settings before the machine cleans and returns to its dock. Whether that removes hand-wiping depends on the actual boundaries in a home, rather than the pad’s outline alone. Model distinctions also matter: the R3’s advertised ultraviolet stain detection should not be assigned to the R2. A manufacturer’s explanation establishes design intent, not the result in every room.

Tangle resistance and pet-hair pickup need separate checks

Vacuum Wars’ November 6 review reported no tangling in its seven-inch hair test, but only 64% pickup of flattened pet hair and avoidance of five out of twenty-four objects. The outlet bought its unit; the results still describe its conditions rather than household-wide failure probabilities. For a buyer, the distinction between the two hair tasks is concrete: less brush disassembly does not necessarily mean less follow-up vacuuming. A home with charging cables, socks or toys should count floor preparation as part of the operating routine. Navigation sensors alone do not establish that someone can start a run without checking the floor.

A fixed carpet and object set give the comparison a scope

The outlet’s published method separates carpet cleaning, hair pickup, tangling and object avoidance, while using a fixed floor plan for navigation comparisons. That separation clarifies which question a number answers: removing hair pressed into fibres, preventing strands from wrapping around a brush, or avoiding a cable. A studio average is a reference drawn from that outlet’s sample, not an industry acceptance threshold. A reader can map a recurring problem at home to the relevant test instead of allowing one aggregate score to make the decision. Different carpets, hair lengths or objects remain reasons to expect results to vary.

The dock reduces repeated actions within a larger care routine

The R2’s bagless collection, hot-water pad washing and warm-air drying can reduce repeated emptying, hand-washing and drying steps. Refilling clean water, handling dirty water and checking blockages remain part of ownership. A practical trial could follow several runs and note residue, successful emptying and every point requiring a person. For a product team, clear indications of tanks needing attention or interrupted maintenance may be more useful than a generic completion notification. This is a proposed evaluation, not an improvement measured here. Regional price, service and consumables still need checking, and an older announcement cannot establish current stock.
