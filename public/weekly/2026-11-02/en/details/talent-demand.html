AI interaction requirements name concrete failure states

StackAdapt and Andgo specify explanations, confidence, recovery and human override: the practical question is how a person takes control when work becomes uncertain. Not every role at an AI company makes AI expertise mandatory; HouseSigma emphasizes mobile B2C research and end-to-end experience. A relevant portfolio can show how a real error was discovered, how the user corrected it and what changed. A chat surface alone does not demonstrate those abilities.

Frontend literacy, handoff and production code are distinct

Astell values frontend literacy and React experience, Andgo describes Figma/MCP delivery, and Alpaca explicitly includes production frontend code, testing and deployment. Treating all three as a designer who knows some code obscures the responsibility difference. Life360’s experiments and Tailscale’s activation work also require interpreting outcomes. Clarify who maintains code, who evaluates an experiment and whether the designer owns production release.

Contract does not mean asynchronous or worldwide

Andgo’s contract asks for four to five days per week and time-zone overlap. Hello Robo requires at least four hours with New York each day, with its fixed-offset wording needing clarification. DEFCON requires US citizenship and active clearance; Harvey is three office days in Toronto, and Flex currently lists New York. Screen eligibility and capacity before comparing pay. Unknown currency, period or contracting arrangements cannot be filled from similar roles.

This is a role sample, not a hiring-growth measure

The list contains seventeen entries across sixteen organizations, including one consulting RFP. These are not seventeen newly posted jobs this week: fifteen were rechecked from existing records and two were newly discovered for this issue; original dates are unverified except the RFP. Advertising is not confirmed hiring. Without a comparable historical denominator, the sample cannot establish growth in AI-design demand. It shows the responsibilities these employers currently describe.
