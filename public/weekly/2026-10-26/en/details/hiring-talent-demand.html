Designing AI behavior differs from using AI tools

StackAdapt asks for explanations, confidence, recovery and a role in defining evals. Andgo focuses on how schedulers see and override decisions; RaiseDesk places approval points inside fundraising workflows. These duties involve turning machine judgments into states a person can act on. Using coding or image tools to accelerate design is a separate capability. Generating an interface does not establish an ability to explain an incorrect business decision. When comparing roles or work samples, separate evidence of how AI helped make the artifact from evidence of how users understand a result, reject an action or resume unfinished work.

Code responsibility extends beyond a prototype label

Astell emphasizes tokens, components and frontend literacy. Alpaca explicitly extends the scope into production review, testing and deployment; Samsara also names code contributions to design systems, web and mobile products. The useful distinction is who maintains the code and at which quality gate the designer hands it over. A working demo does not establish handling of permissions, malformed data or regressions. Exploratory prototypes, implementable specifications and maintained production work deserve separate descriptions. The same employers also ask for research, customer communication and systems judgment, so these advertisements do not support a blanket conclusion that every designer must become a frontend engineer.

Evidence of outcomes should fit the task

Life360 centers on subscription hypotheses and experiments, HouseSigma values mobile research and process, and Samsara asks for work spanning product areas. Impact need not use the same number: one project may concern trial conversion, another scheduling errors, another the inspection of complex data. Without before-and-after measurement, state the observation scope rather than invent a savings percentage. AI language in a vacancy does not establish a common assessment standard or a compensation premium. The public advertisements describe desired delivery; they do not show completed hiring, actual team allocation or post-hire outcomes.

Read engagement form together with cost and schedule

Andgo’s contract asks for four to five days weekly. APF’s consultancy procurement uses milestone acceptance and requires insurance plus a quote covering research expenses. The contract label does not establish schedule freedom, while a procurement ceiling is not personal income. Harvey’s office requirement belongs alongside its title. The thirteen records include one procurement brief and one hybrid role, rather than thirteen Canadian remote full-time jobs. Only APF has a verified posting date, October 22. This sample compares twelve organizations’ advertised requirements; changes in discovery coverage, page accessibility and withdrawals prevent a hiring-growth inference.
