Different sensors handle visibility and distance

Night inspection creates two separate problems: recognizing a target in the image and detecting surrounding obstacles such as thin wires. Matrice 5T combines rotating LiDAR, directional mmWave radar and fisheye vision, alongside native 1280 by 1024 thermal imaging. A temperature contrast can point to something worth inspecting; it does not by itself establish a fault. Enhanced imagery should also not be confused with the sensor’s native resolution. The hardware gives flight and observation different kinds of input. An operator still needs to relate a thermal anomaly to visible details, location and field records before handing it to someone who will decide what repair is needed.

Search conditions narrow the review task

Onboard algorithms inspect visible and thermal feeds together, with DJI claiming more than twenty target categories. Connecting FlightHub 2 enables text or image searches in Pilot 2. Operators can also outline an area and assign actions such as hovering or image capture. This adds task-level inputs beyond a joystick or individual waypoint, while still requiring a clearly scoped mission. More detected candidates could reduce continuous visual watching, but could also create more false alarms to review. An inspection team should track actionable findings and missed anomalies rather than the number of detection boxes. That is a proposed evaluation, not a flight trial completed for this issue.

Outputs need a place in the inspection workflow

DJI also connects field imagery to mapping: the controller can build point clouds or a 2.5D model from received photographs, while Terra can assemble thermal maps and mark anomalies. For repeated inspection of one facility, those outputs become useful when they can be associated with locations, previous records and work orders. External algorithms and development interfaces do not mean an organization’s existing model will run without adaptation. A bounded pilot could revisit a route with known manual records and compare coverage, missing photographs and location errors, retaining original imagery for review. An automatically planned route still needs assessment against the conditions present at that particular site.

Check regional configuration before headline maxima

The FAQ warns that mmWave features may be restricted by region and that the aircraft cannot simply be used across countries; purchasers should confirm the applicable configuration with a dealer. The 59-minute maximum comes from a controlled, unloaded flight to zero battery, not a usable mission-time guarantee. Obstacle sensing also depends on the target and conditions, and DJI says it cannot actively avoid fast-moving objects. Procurement therefore needs to account for local configuration, training, software and maintenance rather than comparing sensor counts alone. This issue verified the announcement and dealer sales route, but not local inventory, a complete quote or recognition success in real operating conditions.
