Inspect automated visits separately

An abandoned checkout leaves several possible explanations: a person changed their mind, the page failed, or an assistant could not find the next action. Agent Activity adds a view of supported automated visits, and simulation assessments exercise commerce and travel tasks. A useful investigation starts with a concrete goal, such as reaching confirmation with the intended item and shipping option. Compare the failing step before and after changing the interface, rather than treating a larger visit count as success. This makes the dashboard relevant to a site owner deciding which control to fix; it is not a general ranking of agent intelligence.

A session can contain a human handoff

Microsoft’s earlier analysis explicitly says an agent-classified session may include a person taking over, because the system cannot currently split those portions. A form submission in that session therefore does not establish autonomous completion. The underlying study also concerns an earlier August-to-September sample, not growth at an individual store this week. For product decisions, treat a recording as a diagnostic lead and reconcile it with the order or appointment system. Reaching checkout, submitting an order, completing payment and cancelling afterward are different outcomes. Combining them would conceal the work a customer still had to do after the agent stopped.

Booking and analysis have different access conditions

For local services, the documented entry is an eligible WordPress site with Clarity, a published Brand Agent and a supported CRM connection. Keeping service coverage and appointment availability accurate remains the business’s job. Ask Clarity serves another task: investigating on-site behavior with recordings behind its advice. Its specific access note says closed beta for retailers, which qualifies the announcement’s broader availability wording. A business should decide whether it needs fault diagnosis, discovery or booking before enabling a module. Those objectives need different outcome measures, and a persuasive answer in a conversation does not itself establish a completed service reservation.

[S32]

Check capture scope before interpreting causes

Clarity’s data documentation describes interaction events, diagnostics and DOM changes used for playback, with masked content for privacy-sensitive fields. These observations reveal behavior rather than a complete account of intent. An analyst can narrow a suspicion into a testable question, open the relevant recordings and check whether missing events, masking or an off-site payment step distort the explanation. Clarity is free according to its overview, but integration, review and fixes still require team effort. No authenticated test here establishes detection accuracy, simulation reliability or booking success. Vendor feedback surveys should not substitute for measuring the particular problem the site owner intends to repair.
