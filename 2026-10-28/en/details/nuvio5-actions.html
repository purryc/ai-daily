Review the intended entry

Asking which invoice remains unpaid and asking an assistant to create an invoice are different interactions. Nuvio5 adds the latter: it resolves a request against existing records and presents the proposed entry before saving. A payment that needs a bank transaction requires the person to choose that transaction. The review object is therefore a concrete record, not simply a confident conversational answer. This can remove copying between a chat and a form, while leaving the owner responsible for checking the amount and counterparty. The official release is dated October 23. Its October 27 Product Hunt appearance is a discovery point, not evidence that the version first became available that day.

An external agent works in a selected company

An outside agent signs in and selects a company, with access constrained by the member’s role. The connection list exposes the company, recent use and a disconnect control. These details are especially relevant to someone maintaining several businesses. An interface that remembers the previous company can be convenient and still require deliberate review before the next write. A practical setup check would use a harmless test record, disconnect the connection and verify what happens to a subsequent request. That is an evaluation suggestion, not a claim that revocation was tested here. The vendor describes duplicate-entry protection, but its coverage across retries and interrupted requests has not been independently established.

Software and professional services are separate

The pricing page distinguishes App Only, App and Bookkeeping, and Bookkeeping plus Tax. It displays monthly amounts of $55, $249 and $549; the retrieved text did not establish a currency code, so these should not be converted into a local-currency quotation. Professional services target US and Canadian startups, with filing scope agreed before subscription. Software-only customers can retain their own accountant. This distinction changes the purchase decision: a founder may want faster expense capture without replacing an existing adviser. Conversely, someone buying bookkeeping needs clarity about the service deliverable, not just the interface. The home page’s combined proposition should not be read as every accounting service being included in every plan.

Keep exceptions attached to the work

The everyday workflow includes exceptions: one payment may cover several invoices, a connection may expire, and older records may need attention before current reporting makes sense. Nuvio presents scanning, matching and financial questions in one workspace. The practical question is whether an exception stays attached to its record when another person takes over. Otherwise, the team may recreate the same explanation in email. Existing bank tools, spreadsheets and accounting software remain relevant alternatives. Running a complete close cycle is more informative than timing one chat request: include review, corrections and communication with the accountant. Maintaining two inconsistent sets of records could consume the time saved by a faster entry interface.
