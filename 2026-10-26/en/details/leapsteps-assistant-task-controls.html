After the conversation, the task record changes

Windows 1.0.3 preserves a running timer after the app closes, clears a completed task’s reminder and refreshes reminders after changes elsewhere. Planning tomorrow in chat only helps if the task list carries the new dates. Direct editing removes the subsequent copying step. It also makes ambiguity consequential: “move the report” needs an identifiable report and a date. A list may contain a weekly draft, a client report and an older reminder at once. The useful confirmation is the changed record, where the person can inspect which commitment moved.

Edit one item while keeping the list visible

Windows places task details beside the list and keeps projects in the sidebar. Multitask groups items handed to AI, shows elapsed time and supports an always-on-top window. Editing one item leaves neighboring commitments visible for comparison. Elapsed time, however, describes waiting rather than progress through drafting, research or approval. A person can continue another task and periodically open the delegated output. The list preserves where the work belongs; the output supplies the evidence of progress. A completed status may still precede proofreading or acceptance by someone else.

[S05]

Confirm the objects before clearing the list

Removal requests name the assistant and tasks. Archives can be restored for seven days; deleted tasks cannot. Consider asking to clear unused ideas: some may be abandoned, while others simply belong later in the calendar. Reviewing the named items exposes that distinction before action. A task may also contain a reference, an unresolved decision or a commitment involving someone else. The approval applies to those particular objects. It should not be interpreted as permission for the next batch of edits, even when the conversational request sounds similar.

[S02]

The goal gives direction; the step must fit today

Goals can be written manually; AI suggestions can be kept, changed or skipped. Tasks, plans and timers work offline, while AI and voice input require a connection. A broad aim such as improving a portfolio still needs a boundary suited to the available materials and time. Organizing one case and rebuilding a whole website are different commitments. Suggestions supply possible steps, with the person choosing their scope. Offline task management allows that planning to continue independently of whether a connected generation request is useful or available.

[S03]

Check where the list will appear before switching devices

Support says Free shows content on the device where it was created; Pro exposes earlier tasks across devices after a switch or reinstall. Connecting an assistant requires LeapSteps sign-in and Allow. Someone capturing work at an office computer and completing it at home therefore needs synchronization as part of the workflow. A person staying on one machine may not. Following one real task through creation, revision and a device switch shows whether its date and context survive the handoff before more work is moved into the system.
