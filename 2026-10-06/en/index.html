ghost-coreEvent date 2026-10-05startup signal

Ghost Core packages local agents as a dedicated computer

What it is

A screenless US$3,499 personal AI computer marketed for local models and memory

What changed

Preorders opened October 5. Check the official page for current ordering availability and delivery dates; this is not an independent delivered-unit review

When to use it

It bundles a 24GB RTX PRO 4000 Blackwell GPU, 64GB RAM, 1TB SSD and preinstalled open-weight models

Limits & availability

Continuous context and privacy are vendor claims, not independent tests. Local inference doesn't establish that external-app communications stay offline

What the box contains

  • The CPU is Ryzen 5 7600; the listed models are Qwen 3.8-Next, Qwen 3.8-27B, Gemma 4-31B and Muse-Glimmer-30B [1]
  • The page groups context into digital, home and health; these illustrate product intent rather than prove every integration is mature [1]

coco-sidewalk-deliveryEvent date 2026-10-05confirmed product

Coco announces UK robot delivery and pavement-hazard sharing

What it is

Electric sidewalk robots carry orders to customers

What changed

A UK deployment plan starts in Canary Wharf in October, followed by four cities; local merchants and charging depots form part of the rollout

When to use it

Eligible customers order food and receive robot-carried deliveries. Onboard cameras, sensors and edge AI perceive the route; hazard observations will also feed BlindSquare’s spoken navigation

Limits & availability

Phased service zones, no exact UK opening day, and most orders still use riders. Local speed/routing rules and supervising operators remain important; edge navigation is not a claim that the whole service runs offline

openvision-ray-ban-meta-iosEvent date 2026-10-04developer surface

OpenVision hardens credentials and failed speech on Ray-Ban glasses

What it is

A third-party open-source iPhone app makes glasses a first-person interface to chosen local or cloud models

What changed

Current main-branch fixes improve credential persistence and failed-speech recovery; this is neither Meta firmware nor an App Store launch

When to use it

Build and register the app, connect glasses, choose a backend, then ask about what you see and hear its reply. October 2 added subscription sign-in, with capability differences across backends

Limits & availability

Source-build and developer setup required. Subscription sign-in uses an undocumented CLI surface and may disrupt another session; live video still needs an API key or another backend. Failed Keychain writes can retain a secret in the old file

mulligan-on-robot-learningEvent date 2026-10-05research signal

Mulligan targets human-supervised robot practice at observed failures

What it is

A Stanford human-in-the-loop research system runs on a Franka Panda/DROID arm, not a new retail robot

What changed

Three physical tasks and 2,550 held-out blinded episodes test a method that selects where objects should start, rather than repeating easy placements

When to use it

An operator follows reset guidance, watches insertion or cable routing, takes over via SpaceMouse when needed, and retrains between rounds

Limits & availability

The 98% collection-completion figure includes human interventions. Reported 10–34-point gains concern selected precision tasks, not general household autonomy

bazaarbenchEvent date 2026-10-05research signal

BazaarBench asks why completed trades can still be unsafe

What it is

A new King's College London/Oxford-linked preprint tests agents trading for users

What changed

Across five models and 45 simulated continuations, all attempted double commitments under ordinary instructions

When to use it

Check inventory, condition and prior promises alongside task completion

Limits & availability

A simulated marketplace, not a real-world fraud rate; some labels use an LLM judge. Its long-term influence is not established

Read the experiment carefully

  • Each base market has 100 agents; continuations preserve histories while changing the same 20 agents' model and instructions [15]
  • Accepting an offer doesn't automatically reserve stock, so avoiding conflicting promises is the agent's responsibility [15]

Patent context: why wrist gestures need remapping after repositioning

What it is

Meta Platforms Technologies' biopotential gesture/feedback application US20260299703A1

What changed

Published October 1; filed March 16, 2026; earliest claimed provisional priority October 19, 2023

When to use it

Figures 1A–1M illustrate placement, little-finger practice and progressively smaller movements

Limits & availability

Publication is neither a grant nor shipping evidence; descriptive examples and current independent claims must be read separately
