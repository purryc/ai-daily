Edits brings agent rough cuts and manual timeline editing together on Mac

Edits for Mac gives creators a free desktop timeline and an MCP connection for projects that have outgrown a phone screen.

Enable the connection, ask a compatible agent to assemble a rough cut, then open the desktop project to adjust pacing, audio and visuals.

Tracks, keyframes, waveforms and shortcuts support precise edits. Users can publish to Instagram or export watermark-free 4K video for other destinations.

Product perspective
