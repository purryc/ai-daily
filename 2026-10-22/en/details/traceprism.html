One partition can hide different problems

An enterprise log can mix straightforward payments, repeated appeals and unresolved cases. If an analyst defines similarity only through activity sequences at the beginning, timing or business attributes may never influence the partition. TracePrism makes that choice part of the interaction: inspect the working set, select an encoding, form a candidate, examine coherence in both structure and outcome, and continue with the remaining cases after acceptance. The intended users are process analysts, process owners and supporting data scientists. The October 20 submission is a three-day supplement; disclosure of a research prototype does not establish a dependable enterprise service.

Reopen the conclusion without losing its basis

Candidates can come from topics, clusters over topic weights or a manually brushed embedding region. Two-dimensional placement assists selection; scoring still uses the full representation. The knowledge view retains membership, working set, encoding and revision history, restores those conditions when a group is reopened, and exports provenance with CSV records. One reported revision reexamines 860 traces, retains 528 in a tighter group and returns 332 to the remainder. Recovery therefore preserves the explanation before and after a change instead of replacing it with a prettier diagram. Client-side processing and background computation are implementation claims that were not independently executed here.

Coverage depends on analytical choices

Usage scenarios cover real traffic-fine, sepsis-care and IT-incident logs. Accepted groups contain 97.3%, 87.2% and 54.3% of their respective records. These are not classifier accuracies: thresholds were adjusted by inspection, and commitment order changes later working sets. Some outcomes are defined by the activities themselves, making structure–outcome agreement partly circular; unresolved cases can also reflect the observation period ending. Seed, topic-count and projection stability were not exhaustively tested, and the paper does not establish superiority over competing methods. The supplement site was inaccessible in this check, leaving the code license and a complete reproducible deployment unverified.

Measure explanation alongside analytical effort

Fixed clustering, manual rules, spreadsheet filtering and conventional process diagrams remain alternatives. TracePrism’s hypothesis is that analysts can change perspective after understanding part of the data while preserving a record colleagues can inspect. A pilot should hold the log and business question constant, ask a second analyst to reopen findings, and verify membership, encoding, thresholds and excluded records. Measure time spent locating a problem and revising its explanation. Temporal concentration alone should not become a causal claim about policy without separate business evidence. More groups are unhelpful if nobody can reconstruct their acceptance; preserved reasoning and less repeated work would support adoption. Unassigned records should remain visible, so difficult minority cases are not silently removed from process-improvement decisions simply because the current representation cannot explain them.
