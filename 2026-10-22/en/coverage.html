Eight lanes: scans, exclusions and gaps

OFFICIAL · Original releases, documentation and implementation checked

The official lane separated release headlines from actual changes. Claude Security’s continuous checks, fix tracking and migration omissions came from its announcement and support documentation. ONEKEY’s first-version release statement was compared with its September roadmap and October 14 feature article, preventing the same capabilities from being presented as newly disclosed. Google Agents CLI uses the October 20 release time for version1.11.0 rather than treating an older extension mechanism in the next day’s tutorial as new. Hardware sources covered manufacturing prototypes, an in-car assistant, warehouse operation and input calibration, distinguishing a 2030 target, concept demonstrations, regional delivery and reported operating deployments. The Nebius returns-training loop already appeared in yesterday’s Sereact analysis and was excluded; Lenovo retail coverage belonged to the earlier RTX Spark launch family. Date-only October 21 sources were conservatively treated as supplements. Syndication time did not replace original publication time. Pricing, platform-support and recovery conflicts remain visible. The team did not perform authenticated execution, robot deployment or performance replication, so vendor documentation remains attributed evidence rather than an independent product test.

REVIEWS · Actual reports read; limited samples and independent-source boundaries retained

The review scan distinguished actual operation, technical investigation, and retelling of announcements. The October 21 LiteLLM incident has a maintainer timeline and two external technical lines from Endor Labs and Chainguard. Chainguard also sells protected images, so its detection speed and customer protection remain attributed commercial claims rather than measurements reproduced here. The Ofox comparison disclosed five AI-assisted task tests and seven documentation-only comparisons, while including its own product. That methodology does not support presenting its ranking as neutral purchasing advice. An original phone-review lead went to the hardware researcher for a full read, and a MiniMax model review went to the China-product researcher for counterchecking. Trail of Bits supplied inspectable versioned code, workflow documentation, and report definitions for its patching and regression tools; its performance figures remain the authors’ preliminary evaluation. The useful outcome is a distinction between claims a reader can inspect and conclusions still requiring independent execution. Search snippets and inaccessible articles were retained as discovery records, without being upgraded into hands-on testing.

COMMUNITY · Actual reports read; limited samples and independent-source boundaries retained

The community scan found concrete mismatches between execution state and what an operator understood. One Collie operator described confusing queue states and a hard-to-find manual-send action across multiple sessions. A separate pi multiline-input report showed why text appearing in a terminal does not prove that it was submitted. Its proposed fix remains open and must not be described as fully shipped. The stable 1.23.2 release does change question cards and manual confirmation for sensitive first lines, which can be checked against maintainer notes. An OpenAI Community dictation complaint came from one author describing delayed sentence endings and punctuation on Windows with Chrome. The official FAQ confirms reviewable text before sending but does not establish a universal redesign date. Three posts about security review and truncation also came from a single author. Both threads remain observations rather than general platform-failure findings. Community evidence is useful for selecting state transitions to test, provided repeated posts are not counted as independent users and one workflow is not converted into a prevalence estimate.

WILD · Maker launches and releases checked; attention is not launch time

The startup and open-source lane read the seventeen entries on the public October 21 Product Hunt board, then checked websites, version histories, code and licenses. BAND, Agent Pets, ilha and The Brains expose different approaches to working across tools, with availability bounded by their own documentation. Earlier public material for ilha and The Brains is retained as a seven-day lookback. ProjectOps is scoped to its4.72.0 release, while RAGFlow is described as a release candidate rather than an established universal deployment. SuperCMO already existed; only the 0.1.27 installation and execution-control changes qualify as new evidence. Earlier Flagsmith beta and marketing-product material prevent a later listing from resetting launch dates. Original galleries were inspected individually: typography posters, promotional codes and animation frames containing only an unfinished command were not accepted as substantive visuals. Public code, software licensing, external model fees and deployability remain separate questions. A discovery board supplies no sales, retention or willingness-to-pay measurement. Relationship reminders, paid messaging and virtual-machine tools without a verified relevant AI mechanism were retained as scope decisions rather than added to fill a category.

RESEARCH · Original papers read

This lane checked recent arXiv human–computer interaction and robotics listings, then read methods, results, limitations and captions for nine papers tied to concrete product tasks. Author repositories and dataset entrances were checked where available. Candidates cover collaborative search, spatial-app acceptance, privacy preferences, purchase permission, robot teleoperation, process analysis, virtual patients, tutoring-video understanding and gaze authentication. Dates refer to submission history, not conference acceptance or collection time. The common shift is from plausible output toward inspectable actions, state and recovery evidence. Results retain the authors’ evaluation units and were not independently reproduced. Public code is distinguished from a deployable web product, human ratings from automated scores, and short controlled tasks from sustained use. The publishing thread separately accepts original visuals; research figures do not become commercial-interface proof. Conceptual discussions, questionnaires and pure control improvements without a clear interaction contribution remain scan findings rather than being expanded into projects merely to increase the count.

PATENT · Watch; original figure and family gaps

The patent lane searched AI interfaces, smart glasses and the specified dates, then traced recent reports to publication numbers, filing dates, priority, independent claims and drawings. A complete Justia republication allowed checking the text of Meta application US20260321292A1. It was published on October 15 and filed on April2,2026, claiming two provisional priorities, April9 and April25,2025; the earlier date must not be lost by copying a secondary report. The disclosure starts with a capture command, aligns audio and image segments, transcribes audio and selects highlights. It is an application disclosure, not evidence of a shipped feature. The official PDF was inaccessible, drawings and the complete family remain unverified, and the precise publication time at the seven-day boundary is unknown, so the item stays on watch. A separate Apple health-sensing-eyewear report led only to a 2024 publication without an identified new grant number. Old patents whose anticipated expiration dates matched the search were excluded as false freshness signals.

CHINA · Regional scope and original sources checked

The China scan followed 36Kr, SSPAI, JiQizhixin and Qbit leads back to original material, identifying Baidu DuMate's enterprise workflow update and Phoenix's technical report with a public pretraining implementation. DuMate's useful control evidence comes from official task, automation and account documentation; customer savings presented at an event are not independent measurements. Phoenix's world model, benchmark tables and physical demonstrations require separate interpretation, and data and real-time deployment components remain forthcoming. Official history corrected recent Yuanbao Search headlines: the API already existed and its rename occurred on October 9, so a newer news date did not justify another first-launch story. MiniMax M3.1 Flash has a verified model and pricing announcement, but no separately established new interaction or API change qualified here. Polibrain lacks sufficient original timing and accepted visual evidence; a Motubrain training livestream does not establish model availability. SSPAI's YoooClaw, Rand/0 and Baimiao PDF storefront entries have older histories. A security check on 36Kr, login-only JiQizhixin article shells and some Qbit retrieval failures limit coverage; this is not an exhaustive account of China's market.

GLOBAL · Regional scope and original sources checked

The global comparison uses OPPO and TorchTPU as concrete examples of different adoption boundaries. Find X10's official global event falls at 14:00 UTC on October 21, but the earlier China debut, preview and Manus partnership are background. UK deposit conditions, staggered availability, account requirements and Call Guard's restriction to India, Indonesia and Thailand show why a global name does not provide every buyer with the same capability package. TorchTPU changes the developer route from PyTorch to TPU, with an open backend, Hugging Face integration and a preview TPU agent suite representing different delivery layers of one project. Partner compatibility testing is not independent performance validation, and a small code change does not remove the need to compare numerical precision, compilation delays and actual compute bills. The lane searched 41 available manifests within the requested eight-week interval and checked the prior issue's candidate state. Dates are not continuous, so missing daily records were not treated as completed deduplication. Syndication and regional pages can share one issuer; a larger link count does not establish more independent confirmations.
