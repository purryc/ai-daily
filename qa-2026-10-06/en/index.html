What it is

A new King's College London/Oxford-linked preprint tests agents trading for users

What changed

Across five models and 45 simulated continuations, all attempted double commitments under ordinary instructions

When to use it

Check inventory, condition and prior promises alongside task completion

Limits & availability

A simulated marketplace, not a real-world fraud rate; some labels use an LLM judge. Its long-term influence is not established
